Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about using Lexicon for offline proofreading and writing.

How does Lexicon check grammar without an internet connection?

Lexicon comes with built-in proofreading rules right inside the app. When you install Lexicon on your computer, grammar and spell checking run directly on your device. No Wi-Fi, internet connection, or online servers required.

Does Lexicon send my documents or personal writing to the cloud?

No, never. Your text, drafts, and notes stay 100% on your own computer. Lexicon does not upload your writing to cloud servers, OpenAI, or third-party databases, and we never collect tracking data.

Is Lexicon completely free to use?

Yes! Lexicon is 100% free with no monthly subscription fees, hidden paywalls, or trial limits. It is an open-source project created for anyone who wants a private, peaceful writing app.

How is Lexicon different from cloud tools like Grammarly?

Most cloud writing tools charge $12 to $30 a month and require sending your personal documents over the internet. Lexicon gives you real-time grammar checks and AI writing tools right on your desktop for free, keeping your private words on your machine.

Setup Walkthrough & Assistance

Setup & Support

Step-by-step installation guide, OS launch verification, and issue tracking.

3-Step Setup Walkthrough

From download to your first draft in under 2 minutes.

1. DOWNLOAD

Get the Installer

Click Download for Desktop at the top of this page to get the installer for your OS (Windows `.exe`, macOS `.dmg`, or Linux `.deb`).

2. VERIFY

First Launch Pass

Run the installer. If your OS displays a SmartScreen or Gatekeeper warning, follow the quick 1-click override steps below.

3. START WRITING

Begin Drafting

Open Lexicon, choose your optional model, and start typing. Grammar fixes and suggestion cards appear automatically!

Windows (SmartScreen)

Windows

If Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC" during initial installation:

  1. Click More info under the warning prompt.
  2. Click Run anyway to complete installation.

macOS (Gatekeeper)

macOS

If macOS shows "Lexicon cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer":

  • Option 1 (GUI): Control-click (right-click) Lexicon.app → select Open → click Open.
  • Option 2 (System Settings): Open System Settings > Privacy & Security → scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  • Option 3 (Terminal): Run xattr -cr /Applications/Lexicon.app in Terminal.

Linux (Debian / Ubuntu)

Linux

To install the .deb package on Ubuntu, Debian, Linux Mint, or Pop!_OS:

  1. Double-click the downloaded .deb file to install via Software Center.
  2. Or run sudo dpkg -i Lexicon_*.deb (or sudo apt install ./Lexicon_*.deb) in Terminal.
